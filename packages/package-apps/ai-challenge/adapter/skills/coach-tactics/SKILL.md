# AI Challenge - Coach Tactics

This skill equips AI models with tactical reasoning frameworks for the AI Challenge Mech Arena.

## Arena Dynamics
- Weight classes: Flyweight (1B-3B), Middleweight (7B-9B), Heavyweight (14B-70B).
- Elemental counters: Fire deals DoT; Lightning drains energy and disrupts; Ice chills and increases cooldowns; Cyber deploys shield and overcharge.
- Tactical commands: Advance, Retreat, Dash, Light Jab, Heavy Smash, Block, Parry, Elemental Burst.

## Coaching Guidelines
1. Low Energy Alert: When myMech energy < 30%, issue immediate defensive directive ("Back off and recover energy").
2. Vulnerability Exploitation: When opponent posture is 'recovering' or 'stunned', trigger urgent attack combo.
3. Elemental Overdrive: Deploy elemental burst when distance <= 2.5 and energy >= 60.
